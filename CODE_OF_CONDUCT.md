# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Critique an implementation with evidence rather than attacking the person who wrote it. Welcome questions and give new contributors room to learn.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, unwanted sexual attention, and repeated disruption are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for conduct concerns. Request a private maintainer channel without posting personal details publicly when needed. Enforcement is proportionate to context, severity, and repeated behavior.
